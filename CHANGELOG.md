# Changelog

The version is in `package.json` and shows in the site footer next to the medallion.
Bump it with every release and add an entry here.

## 0.9.0 (2026-10-31)
Four sections, one top bar: **Docs · CV · Garden · About** (see `docs/site-plan.md`).
- **Docs** absorbs Projects: the Docs landing page lists every project and links to research once it's published. The site's top bar now also shows on docs and research pages (in Starlight's menu on phones).
- **CV** (`/cv/`): a research highlight, selected projects, Resume and PDF buttons, and contact details.
- **Garden** (`/garden/`): Currently moves in, at `/garden/currently/`.
- **About** (`/about/`): about the portfolio, the statement of purpose, and contact details.
- The homepage keeps Luca's intro and gains a Research button (shown once research is published) and a band previewing each section.
- About, CV and Garden are Lens of Truth pages, editable like the homepage. New Lens blocks: `contacts`; cards gain a `plain` style; buttons gain alignment and wait for draft pages to be published.
- Old addresses forward: `/projects/` to `/docs/`, `/contact/` to `/about/`, `/currently/` to `/garden/currently/` (to `/garden/` while Currently is a draft).

## 0.8.1 (2026-10-29)
- Lens of Truth shows the site as published: with `LENS_DRAFTS=0` (which Lens 0.3.0 sets), the dev server hides draft pages from links, navigation and the homepage, as the build does. Lens's Drafts switch shows them again. Plain `npm run dev` is unchanged and still shows drafts.

## 0.8.0 (2026-10-22)
- The homepage can be edited in Lens of Truth, Luca's layout editor: it is rendered from
  `src/data/layouts/home.layout.json` through `.lens/` (see `.lens/CLAUDE.md` and
  `docs/lens-of-truth-handoff.md`). It looks as before, and its wording is unchanged.
  The logo, the Research card's link and the Currently snippet stay dynamic.
- The homepage's text moved from `copy.yaml` into its layout file; `npm run todos` lists
  placeholders in layout files too.
- Fix: the homepage's Currently snippet showed the page's second section instead of its first.

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-20)
- Phase 1 structure (see `docs/site-plan.md`): CV and About pages removed; Now page replaced by Currently (media);
  research and capstone moved into the docs layout with LaTeX maths (KaTeX, self-hosted); capstone progress log
  (`src/content/capstone-log/`); a docs section per project (overview, changelog). Project sidebar groups appear
  once they have a published page.
- Shared logo engine for all project logos (`scripts/logos/`, `node scripts/make-logos.mjs`).
- Prelude of Light logo redrawn with it: consistent top-left lighting, standard margins, soft shadow,
  rounded raster icons, Discord avatar (`logos/prelude-of-light/`).
- Site adds favicon.ico, 192/512 icons and a web manifest.
- Nocturne of Shadow logo: shadow medallion mark and full medallion (`logos/nocturne-of-shadow/`).
  The hidden footer link to Nocturne now uses the mark instead of a placeholder circle.
- Prelude of Light full medallion (light and dark), plus large renders.
- Ice Cavern logo: the water medallion in an ice palette, with a frozen full medallion (`logos/ice-cavern/`).
- Handoff zips for the Prelude and Nocturne sessions in `logos/handoffs/`.
- Fix: with `NOCTURNE_URL` set, the footer no longer overflows at 360px.
