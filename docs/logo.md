# Forest medallion logo: spec

Status: **in use since 0.6.1**. B (the mark) is used in the site and docs headers, the browser tab, bookmarks, the touch icon and link previews. A (the compact medallion) sits in the footer next to the site version. Both use the mostly low-poly style (`SMOOTHNESS = 0.3`). Use `src/components/Logo.astro` (`variant="mark" | "bare" | "medallion"`) rather than the files directly.

Everything is generated by `scripts/make-logo.mjs` from one blade definition. Change the numbers there, then run `node scripts/make-logo.mjs`, which writes `src/assets/logo/`. Never hand-edit the SVGs.

## Construction (shared)

All coordinates are normalised: plate circumradius = 1, centre at 0,0, y pointing down.

- **Blade.** A crescent with a sharp tip at each end and a ridge down the middle. It runs clockwise from the inner tip (radius 0.165, near the centre) to the outer tip (radius 0.609, angle −4.8°, just above the right-hand horizontal). It is thickest about a third of the way back from the outer tip. Its three edges (outer, ridge, inner) are each defined by 5–7 points fitted to the in-game medallion: the original's three blades were averaged into one.
- **Arrangement.** Three copies of the blade at exact 120° steps, oriented as in the original.
- **Curves.** `SMOOTHNESS` blends between the straight segments of the game model (0, low-poly) and a centripetal Catmull–Rom spline through the same points (1, smooth like the drawn reference). The logo uses 0.3: clearly faceted, corners slightly softened.
- **Depth.** Each blade is a ridge raised 0.075 above its edges and shaded in 64 strips (20 in the compact files).
  - Light comes from the top left and in front: direction (−0.55, −0.7, 0.85).
  - Shading is Lambert diffuse plus a Blinn–Phong highlight (strength 0.3, exponent 22).
  - Result: the outer face of the top blade and the inner face of the lower-right blade catch the light; the faces turned away from it go dark forest green.
- **Tone ramp** (light value → colour), built around the site palette:

  | Value | Colour | Note |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0.00 | `#0a2214` | deepest shadow, rune grooves |
  | 0.20 | `#153a26` | |
  | 0.34 | `#1f4d34` | `--forest` |
  | 0.48 | `#2d6a46` | `--link` |
  | 0.62 | `#3a8650` | |
  | 0.76 | `#5ea866` | |
  | 0.90 | `#a0d388` | highlights |
  | 1.00 | `#e2f2cf` | specular peak, close to `--sage` |

## A · Medallion

- **Plate.** A twelve-gon with a vertex at the top. It has these rings, by circumradius:

  | Ring | Full | Compact |
  |---|---|---|
  | Outer edge | 1.0 | 1.0 |
  | Outer chamfer, sloping outward at 55° | 1.0 → 0.962 | 1.0 → 0.95 |
  | Rune band (flat) | 0.962 → 0.771 | 0.95 → 0.87 |
  | Bevel, sloping inward at 62°, toned 0.12 darker | 0.771 → 0.68 | 0.87 → 0.795 |
  | Recessed face | < 0.68 | < 0.795 |

  These match the proportions of the original.
- **Flat faces.** Each flat area carries a soft diagonal gradient from top left to bottom right. The recessed face is about 0.1 darker than the band.
- **Shadows.** The top-left bevel wall casts a crisp shadow onto the recessed face, offset (0.032, 0.04) × radius at 40%. Each blade casts a shadow at 55% of that offset, at 50%.
- **Blade outlines.** A thin dark edge (0.6% of the radius, 35% opacity) keeps the blades separate from the face.
- **Runes.** Engraved grooves: a `#0a2214` core with a lit lip on the lower right, stroke width 0.019.
  - One quadrant of seven glyphs (arches, a Z, hooks, zigzags), mirrored left/right and top/bottom like the original's band.
  - A ring with a stem sits at the top and bottom centres, and a small box at the left and right.
  - The glyphs are drawn in the spirit of the original, not copied from it.

## B · Mark

- **Tile.** Rounded square, corner radius 22% of the size, vertical gradient `#f4f8ef` → `#e1eada`.
- **Blades.** The outer tips reach 40% of the size from the centre, leaving about 10% margin. The group is optically centred: moved 75% of the way from its rotation centre to its bounding-box centre.
- **Light.** As above, with a softer highlight (0.2) and 0.03 darker overall, so the lit faces don't wash into the light tile.
- **Shadow.** A soft forest shadow under the blades: offset (1.2%, 2%), blur 1.2%, 24% opacity.

## Lockup (both versions)

Logo, then the wordmark "Minuet of Forest" in Hylia Serif, colour `--forest`, matching the Prelude of Light banner:
- Logo height ≈ 1.5 × the wordmark's capital height.
- Gap between logo and wordmark ≈ 0.3 × the logo height.
- Logo and wordmark are vertically centred on each other.
- Keep clear space of a quarter of the logo height around the lockup.

## Don'ts

- Don't recolour outside the tone ramp, or put the logo on a background darker than `--link` without checking contrast.
- Don't rotate or mirror the logo: the blades turn clockwise from the centre outwards.
- Don't use the full medallion below 96 px: use the compact one.
- Keep the logo out of the resume and CV PDFs.
