# Lens of Truth logo

The Lens of Truth with the Sheikah eye. Source: `scripts/logos/lens.mjs`.

- **Frame.** An octagon with a flat top, in faceted purple: outer chamfer, flat band, bevel into the glass.
- **Spikes.** Three red pyramids on the top edges, at the top and 50° either side.
- **Glass.** Violet, with a soft highlight at the top left. The Sheikah eye sits on it in raised crimson: an almond outline and an oval pupil.
- **Handle.** The Sheikah symbol's teardrop, hanging from the frame, with a gold collar.
- **Outline.** A dark purple outline (`#1a0630`) around everything, so it reads on light and dark backgrounds.
