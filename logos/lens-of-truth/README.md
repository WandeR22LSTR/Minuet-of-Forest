# Lens of Truth logo

The Sheikah eye in the Lens of Truth's colours. Source: `scripts/logos/lens.mjs`.

- **Spikes.** The symbol's three triangles as the lens's red spikes: faceted pyramids at the top and 42° either side, clear of the eye.
- **Eye.** Upper lid running on into a curl at each end, lower lid and an oval pupil, as raised ridged strokes in the pinkish red of the eye on the lens's glass.
- **Teardrop.** A stem from the pupil down to the teardrop, in the lens's purple, shaded round.
- **Outline.** A dark purple outline (`#1a0630`) around everything, so it reads on light and dark backgrounds.
