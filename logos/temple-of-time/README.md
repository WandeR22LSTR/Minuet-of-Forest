# Temple of Time logo

The Triforce above the Door of Time, in the temple's grey-blue stone. Source: `scripts/logos/temple.mjs`.

- **Emblem.** The Triforce, side 1, split into three pieces that meet at their corners. Each piece is a low-poly solid: a flat top raised above its outline and joined to it by three narrow bevelled faces close to its edges, lit from the top left like the other project logos.
- **Mark.** The Triforce raised from a pale stone tile (or a dark one), shaded with the same stone tones as the medallion.
- **Medallion.** The shared twelve-sided plate in grey-blue stone, with the Triforce engraved into the face, set slightly below centre with a margin to the bevel. The runes and bevel are lit the same way as the engraving: the lower-right wall of every cut catches the light. Large display only; there is no compact medallion.

| File | Use |

| `discord-avatar-1024.png` | The mark on a square dark tile, sized to clear Discord's circular crop: the bot's avatar |
| `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `site.webmanifest` | Tab fallback, bookmarks, home screen |
